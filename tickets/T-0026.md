# T-0026 - Capture authoritative Hunyuan shape provenance with verified logger

## Goal

Create and verify a dependency-light, append-only, tamper-evident research logger,
then use it to capture authoritative Hunyuan3D 2.1 shape-model provenance from only
official immutable public endpoints. Record an exact request/response log with a
verified hash chain and produce an operator-review-gated production shape manifest,
its provenance document, an offline validator, and human-readable evidence. No
acquisition is authorized.

## Context

T-0023 established the immutable Hunyuan shape-model binding and offline cache
preflight policy but deliberately deferred the real official production manifest
and acquisition/license procedure. T-0024 and T-0025 were earlier unmerged attempts
to capture this provenance. Both were closed without merge after independent review:
T-0024 had incomplete request-byte accounting and incomplete immutable loader line
evidence, and T-0025 had a 404 request record manually inserted after the logger
failed to record non-2xx responses.

T-0026 is a clean retry from current `main`. The old branches are audit references
only and are not reused as authoritative evidence.

## Implementation notes

Phase A adds a standard-library-only research logger with append-only JSONL storage,
hash-chain verification, request/budget enforcement, fail-closed `Content-Length`
handling, redirect-hop accounting, and offline tests using local fake HTTP servers.
Only after Phase A is committed and green may the bounded public research session
run. The session plan reserves at least one request slot and is frozen before the
first request. The logger finalizes the session and refuses further appends after
verification.
